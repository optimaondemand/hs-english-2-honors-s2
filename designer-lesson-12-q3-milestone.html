Lesson 12
Q3 Milestone
Quarter 3 comes together. You show that your game has a finished look drawn from real sources, a Rules Document a stranger can follow, and a pitch you can say aloud in two to three minutes.
YOUR PATH TODAY
Gather the milestone→Write the pitch→Record it→Get AI feedback→Assemble and turn in
You will need:About 90 minutes, plus recording timeLessons 9 to 11 and your playtest logsAny device that records audio or video
Your last name
1
Gather the milestone
A finished look, tested rules
●
Gather what you already have. Then describe the finished look and say where each major choice came from.
My finished look, and where it comes from
Name the sources behind the color, dress or shapes, lettering, and sound. Say what is made and finished.
The Midsummer lens in my game, as it stands now
One sentence: hidden roles or transformation used, or rejected.
Rules Document: what changed since playtest cycle 2
2
Write the pitch
Five beats, two to three minutes
●
Speak the pitch, do not read an essay. Two minutes is about 280 words and three minutes is about 420 words, so write between 280 and 420. Five beats cover the ground.
1
The question
About 15 seconds: your design question, in one breath.
2
The world and the look
About 30 seconds: your world, and one choice that came from a real source.
3
One turn
About 45 seconds: show the game and play a turn.
4
What testing changed
About 30 seconds: one rule that failed and how you fixed it.
5
What it argues
About 20 seconds: what winning rewards, in your game.
Your pitch script
Write it the way you will say it. Short sentences.
QUICK CHECK · NOT GRADED
Your script runs 700 words. What should you do?
3
Record the pitch and demo
Two to three minutes
●
Record the pitch and a short demo of the game in one take or a few. Any phone, laptop, or tablet works, in audio or video. Show the components or the screen while you speak. Keep it between two and three minutes.
BEFORE YOU PRESS RECORD
Read the script aloud once. Place the components where you can show them. Check that the sound is clear. Say your name and the name of your game first.
The length of my recording and what it shows
For example: 2 minutes 40 seconds, video, shows the cards and one played turn.
4
AI feedback on the pitch
Keep or reject, with reasons
●
Give an AI tool, such as your school Microsoft Copilot account, your script or a transcript. Ask for feedback on clarity and on whether the argument of your game comes through. Keep or reject each point with a reason.
AI AWARENESS AI feedback on a script reads the words only. It cannot judge your voice, pacing, or how the demo looks on screen. Weigh its points against what you hear when you play your recording back.
The AI feedback
What I kept, and why
What I rejected, and why

The pitch feedback step

What it gave me
How I checked or judged it
What I changed, and why
