🎮 DESIGN FOCUS
What winning says about how to live
The Designer’s Table · Quarter 4: the Gatsby lens
A win condition tells players what to want, and so it makes a claim about how to live. Audit yours: what does winning reward, what does it cost the winner, and whom does it leave out? Then read one more scholarly source on how your period’s society understood a good life, and answer an AI that argues your game endorses a hollow value.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Audit your win condition with the Gatsby lens. Log JStor find #4 on the society of your period and how to live well, and rank your four finds for reliability. Get an AI to argue that your game endorses a hollow value, then rebut it with evidence from your rules or revise. Write the revised win condition. The work is done in the Designer’s Table lesson The Win Condition. Complete every tab there. The lesson gathers your answers into a PDF at the end.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
If someone lived the way my game rewards, would that be living well, and what in my rules shows it?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Audit the win condition. State what winning rewards, what it costs the winner, and whom it leaves out, with specific rules.
2
Log JStor find #4. Find a scholarly source on your period’s society and its ideas of a good life. Log the citation, the claim, and how it bears on your game. Rank your four finds.
3
Get the AI challenge. Ask an AI to argue that your game endorses a hollow value, and test its argument against your actual rules.
4
Rebut or revise. Answer the strongest point with specific rules, or change the rules.
5
Write the revised win condition exactly as it will stand in the Rules Document.
6
Log your AI use with one new row in your AI-Use Log.
⚠️ AI-USE REMINDER
An AI will argue any side you ask for, and it can sound sure about points it has not checked. Test its argument against your rules. It may misdescribe your game.
